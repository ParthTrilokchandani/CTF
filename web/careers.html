Careers

We hire deliberately, and rarely.

Sentinel Meridian Group keeps a small team by design. We are not always hiring, but we maintain an ongoing interest in hearing from experienced analysts and researchers who share our approach to rigorous, source-verified work.

Current Openings

Senior Research Analyst

5+ years in due diligence, financial crime investigation, or a related field. Strong written communication required.

Regional Field Associate

Part-time, engagement basis. Local market knowledge and an existing professional network in the relevant region.

What We Look For

Strong candidates typically combine formal research training with direct field experience - former government analysts, corporate security professionals, and investigative journalists have all found a good fit here. We place a high value on intellectual honesty: analysts who are comfortable saying "the evidence doesn't support a firm conclusion" rather than overstating confidence.

How to Apply

There is no online application portal. Interested candidates should reach out through the contact page with a short note on relevant experience. We respond individually to every inquiry, though response times vary with current engagement load.
