Services

Three practice areas, one analytical standard.

Risk Assessment

Our risk assessment engagements combine structured frameworks with on-the-ground judgment. Rather than producing a generic checklist, each assessment is built around the specific operating environment a client faces: regulatory exposure, physical security posture, political stability of the operating region, and counterparty risk are all weighed together rather than scored in isolation.

Typical deliverables include a written risk register, a prioritized mitigation roadmap, and where relevant, a short verbal briefing for senior leadership. Engagements range from a single-location review to a multi-country risk mapping exercise conducted over several months.

Due Diligence

Due diligence work spans corporate registration history, beneficial ownership research where legally disclosable, litigation and regulatory history, and reputational screening across public record sources. We do not rely on a single database or automated screening tool - every finding is corroborated against at least one independent source before it appears in a client deliverable.

This practice area supports pre-transaction reviews, new-partner vetting, and periodic re-screening of existing counterparties as part of an ongoing compliance program.

Advisory Retainers

For clients operating in complex or fast-changing environments, we offer ongoing advisory retainers rather than one-off engagements. A retainer typically includes a standing point of contact, scheduled situational briefings, and the ability to request an ad hoc assessment when conditions change unexpectedly.

Retainer clients also receive priority access to our analyst bench during periods of heightened activity, without needing to negotiate a new statement of work for every request.

Engagement Process

1. Scoping

A structured conversation to define objectives, constraints, and the specific questions the engagement needs to answer.

2. Research & Analysis

Dedicated analyst time, drawing on open-source research, prior engagement archives, and where appropriate, field contacts.

3. Reporting

Findings delivered in plain language, with a clear separation between confirmed fact, informed assessment, and open question.
