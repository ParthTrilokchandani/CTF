Our Analysts

Our analyst bench is intentionally kept small and senior. Each analyst carries a caseload calibrated to allow genuine depth of research on every engagement, rather than being spread thin across a large number of concurrent clients. Analysts are assigned to engagements based on regional and subject-matter fit, not simple availability.

Working With Us

Clients are introduced to their assigned analyst team at the start of an engagement and retain that same point of contact for its duration. We find this continuity produces better research outcomes than a rotating-staff model, particularly for retainer clients whose situational awareness needs evolve gradually over time.
