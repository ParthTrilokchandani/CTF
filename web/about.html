About Us

A small firm, deliberately.

Sentinel Meridian Group was founded by a group of former government analysts and private-sector security consultants who believed that good intelligence work does not require a large footprint. We keep our team small, our client list confidential, and our methods rigorous.

Our Approach

Every engagement begins with a scoping conversation, not a template. We assign a dedicated analyst team, define clear deliverables, and report findings in plain language, free of unnecessary jargon.

Our People

Our analysts have backgrounds spanning diplomatic service, corporate security, journalism, and financial crime investigation. Individual biographies are shared with clients directly during onboarding rather than published publicly.

Our Standards

We operate under a strict internal code of conduct covering conflicts of interest, source protection, and data handling. Internal operational material, historical engagement records, and legacy infrastructure are retained under restricted access in accordance with that policy.
